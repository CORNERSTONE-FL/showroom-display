Architecture begins with the opening.

Founded in 2017, Cornerstone is a family-owned company built on a belief: extraordinary architecture deserves extraordinary fenestration.

Our West Palm Beach showroom brings the Brombal Group to South Florida. Architects, designers, builders and homeowners can see complete installations side by side and settle the decisions that drawings and samples leave open.

The collections

Three materials. One Italian group.

The Brombal Group brings together specialist makers under one roof. Each brand is on the showroom floor, so you can compare them directly.

Steel

Brombal

Luxury metal windows and doors. Slim steel profiles, from divided-lite French doors to full-height glass walls.

Aluminum

ViewScapes

Luxury aluminum windows and doors, launched in 2017. Its Lumina system is the group's first internally engineered minimal frame.

Wood

eWood

Luxury wood windows and doors, launched in 2022 and made by Impronta, a wood window and door maker since 2002.

Glass for the group comes from Zanatta Vetro, which has made glass since 1972 and produced its first jumbo-sized insulated glass unit in 2007.

Heritage

From a metal shop to West Palm Beach.

Half a century of Italian craft, now a short drive from the homes it is made for.

  1. 1970

    Pietro Brombal opens his first metal shop.

  2. 2002

    His sons Leonardo and Pierpaolo join the family business.

  3. 2004

    First US project, in Napa Valley, California.

  4. 2011

    Brombal USA is founded.

  5. 2022

    The Brombal Group is formed.

  6. 2025

    The Opera series, Brombal's first proprietary profile, launches.

  7. 2025

    Cornerstone opens the Brombal Group showroom in West Palm Beach.
